About.

I work on both brand and product, from packaging and print files to user flows and screens in Figma. I like the projects where both have to fit together.

Most of my work so far has been in-house: packaging, sales presentations, Meta Ads campaigns and the odd video edit, usually with a deadline attached. It taught me to ask what a piece is for before deciding how it looks.
