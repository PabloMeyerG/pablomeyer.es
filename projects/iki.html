01 — Overview

IKI —
ZenElegance

IKI is a conceptual fashion brand built around the Japanese idea of iki: understated elegance, simplicity and conscious living.

The identity explores the balance between contemporary minimalism and a quieter, more tactile approach to fashion.

02 — The name

粋

Understated
elegance.

The name comes from iki, a Japanese ideal of style that doesn’t announce itself — authentic, simple, without display. The identity is built to behave the same way.

03 — Principles

Three rules decide what stays — and what goes.

  1. 01

    Minimalism

    Visual simplicity and intentional design.

  2. 02

    Consciousness

    A considered approach to materials, production and consumption.

  3. 03

    Timeless elegance

    A visual language designed to exist beyond short-term trends.

07 — Art direction

Natural light. Neutral rooms. The garment first.

  • Natural light
  • Neutral environments
  • Texture
  • Restrained composition

Casting and styling references selected for the brand book’s photography guide — the brief for every shoot.

08 — Touchpoints

Small objects, same rules.

Business card, thank-you card, label and envelope — black, white and one typeface, so each piece reads as part of the same set.

Woven neck label — the wordmark alone, as the minimum-size rule asks.

09 — Packaging

Open here. Reuse me.

The box speaks twice. Outside, one instruction: open here. Inside, one request: reuse me. Everything else is the material.

Material

10 — Campaign

Three lines. One voice.

A poster family on a shared grid: logo top-left, line bottom, the same rule and folio. Background and crop change; the system doesn’t.

IKI — Collection 01

A lookbook study: cover and two spreads, set in the brand’s monospace with generous margins and a single image per idea.

13 — Digital

The shop, in the same voice.

Oversized wordmark, a single rail of garments and monospaced controls. The e-commerce borrows the print grid instead of a template.

Home
Wordmark at full height, one rail of garments
Collection
Black monospaced filters, three-up grid
Product
Neutral backdrop, garment centred

Nothing on the page is decoration: the logo does the branding, the clothes do the rest. On mobile the rail becomes the hero and the grid drops to two columns.

Tablet — the same home in context.

14 — Social

A feed that reads like a lookbook.

Image, product, type, repeat. Posts alternate so the grid never turns into a catalogue.

On the phone, the profile grid keeps the same rhythm: one image, one product, one line of type.
