About the gallery

The parallel log.

While long projects polish the system, one-offs are the lab. Posters for festivals, conceptual packaging, editorial posts, campaign explorations — each one is an excuse to try a new technique, a different sector or a different way of thinking about brand.

They don't sit on a 40-page brief. They have an idea, a surface and a day. They work as a visual gym: if you don't train the eye when there's nothing on the line, you'll lack range when the big project comes. Here's part of that range.
